01 — About

Telling stories with data.

If you ask me what great marketing is, my answer is simple: a good story.

  • A story that understands its audience.
  • A story that makes people care.
  • A story that turns attention into action.

I started my career as a TV reporter and anchor, covering AI, technology, finance, and business stories for a broad audience. The newsroom taught me how to find the human angle behind complex information, shape it with clarity, and turn it into stories people could understand, remember, and trust.

Later, I moved into CRM and customer strategy, where I began seeing audiences through data. I learned how customer behavior, segmentation, campaign performance, and engagement patterns can reveal what people need, what they respond to, and what drives them to act.

Now, as an MS in Marketing Analytics student at USC Marshall, I bring both worlds together. I use data to find the story, strategy to shape it, and marketing to deliver it in a way that is thoughtful, measurable, and human.
